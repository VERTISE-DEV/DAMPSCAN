# Running the business from ChatGPT

Once the staff area is connected to ChatGPT (or Claude), you can just talk to it. It sees what you see in the staff area and nothing more: your businesses, and money only where you manage the business. Before it changes anything it reads back what it is about to do and waits for you to say yes. Nothing is ever sent to a customer by itself: messages come back as WhatsApp, text and email links for you to tap.

Just say the customer's name, postcode or both ("Mrs Patel", "BR6 0AA", "Patel BR6"). You never need a job number. If two customers match, it describes them and asks which one you mean.

## Catch me up

- "What's happened since I last checked?" / "Catch me up." (new enquiries, bookings and moves, payments and ticks, reports sent, quotes and invoices, deletions and closures, to-dos others set for you, and anything else, each saying who did it, for which customer and when. Your own changes are left out. The first time covers the last 24 hours; after that, since you last asked.)
- "What's happened since yesterday?" / "...in the last 3 hours?" / "...since 2026-10-12?"
- "Catch me up, including my own changes."
- "Anything new? Don't mark it as read." (peek: looks without moving your bookmark)

## What needs doing

- "What needs doing today?" / "What's outstanding?" (the same list as the Calendar's Today panel: today's visits, then every action needed, grouped and counted: enquiries to call back, messages to send, quotes accepted with no date, surveys awaiting the estate agent, reports to send (paid surveys whose report has not gone, red after two days), remedial work to follow up, deposits and payments to collect, overdue invoices, quotes to chase, to-dos, services due, over budget. Money only if you manage the business.)
- "What's outstanding for Verge?" (any company on its own)
- "Show me the full Due list." (the Due page as it was, with visits this week and payouts to freeze)
- "Which messages are due today?"
- "What's on the calendar next week?"
- "Any new enquiries since Monday?"
- "Find Mrs Patel." / "Who's at BR6 0AA?"

## Website enquiries

- "Show me the open enquiries." / "Which enquiries did we close this month?" (each enquiry is open, converted (a job came from it) or closed)
- "Close Sarah's enquiry in BR6, she booked elsewhere." (reasons: booked elsewhere, not interested, spam, duplicate, no response, or your own words; closed ones stop showing in call-backs, half-finished form alerts and the weekly count, but stay on the Leads page greyed)
- "Reopen the Patel enquiry."
- "Delete that spam enquiry from test@example.com." (managers only, it asks you to confirm first, and never for an enquiry a job came from)
- "Book Sarah Jones BR6 in for a survey on Friday." (if she came from a web enquiry ChatGPT links it, so the enquiry shows as converted; close any leftovers)
- If more than one enquiry matches, it describes them and asks which.

## Clients

- "Add a new client for CoolRight: Dev Shah, TN13 1AA, 07700 900456."
- "Mrs Patel's new number is 07700 900999." / "Fix the spelling of the name on Mrs Patel's job to Anita Patel." (works on ATi and DampScan cards too: a phone or email set here is used instead of the enquiry's, handy for surveys booked by phone)
- "Start a new job for Mrs Patel, same details as last time." (it reuses the client, no retyping)

- "Move Mrs Patel to Thursday at 10." / "Push the Smiths in TN13 back a week." (the date moves, nothing else changes)

## Your day, start to finish

- "Brief me for today." / "Brief Tom for Thursday." (each job in time order: customer, full address with a maps link, phone, notes, what's owed if you manage the business, deposit, paid and report ticks, the estate agent, earlier jobs at that postcode in any business, and open to-dos for it)
- "Who's on Thursday?" / "Who's free next week?" (by who the job was handed to, or the surveyor on a damp survey; it does not know about holidays)
- "Hand the Sarah Jones job to Tom." (Tom is put on the job and gets a to-do with the address, date and phone; his phone buzzes)
- "Just rang Sarah in BR6, she wants 2pm instead." (a dated call note on the job, the time moved, and the team hears it in their catch-up) / "Rang Ann in BR1 about her enquiry, ring back Friday."
- "Done at Mrs Patel's: rising damp on the rear wall." (marks the visit done, saves the findings, and puts "Send report for Dee, ME1" on your list for two working days on, or the day you say)
- "Wrap up my day." (visits not marked done, tomorrow's jobs missing a time or address, messages due, reports to send, deposits and balances, overdue invoices, to-dos and unanswered enquiries)
- "How did this week go?" / "How did last week compare?" (enquiries, bookings, money in if you manage, reports sent, reviews, and what is slipping)
- "Everything we've ever done for 07700 900123." / "History for 1 High Street." (every enquiry and job across all your businesses)

## Writing to customers, reports and house style

- "Draft a balance chase for Sarah." / "Write a booking confirmation for Mrs Patel." (it reads the playbook, the job's facts and links, and any ready-made message, then writes it; you get WhatsApp, text and email links for the final words)
- "Give me links to send this to Ola: ..." (links only; nothing is sent until you tap)
- "Write up the report for Mrs Patel and keep it as a draft." / "Get Mrs Patel's report draft back."
- "Remember that we always sign off 'Cheers, Scott'." / "Remember we never quote over the phone." (saved to the shared playbook, so Tom's ChatGPT writes the same way)
- "What's in our playbook?" / "Forget the note about Saturday work."
- "Look at my sent emails and learn how I write." (with your own Outlook connected in ChatGPT, it reads them there, tells you the style it found, and saves it to the playbook once you agree)

## What can it do?

- "What can you do?" / "What can you do for roofing?" (a guide built from everything the connector has, with example phrases)
- Ask for something it cannot do and it says so plainly and offers the nearest thing: it cannot send emails or texts itself (it gives tap-to-send links, or your Outlook connector can send), take card payments (it marks paid), add staff logins (owners use npm run create-person), or delete an enquiry a job came from (it closes it).

## To-do list

A to-do list shared between staff. Ask the assistant to put something on somebody's list and it keeps your words exactly as you said them. It is also in the staff app under **To-do** (for each business, and across all of them from the top bar), with Mine, Set by me, Failed and Done, and a box to tick when it's done.

- Tom: "Need to tell Scott about a potential job next week, name Laura." (a to-do for Scott, in Tom's words, set by Tom; Scott's phone buzzes)
- Scott: "Anything on the to-do list?" (it lists Tom's note, who set it and when; it also checks at the start of every chat)
- "Remind me to order the lead for Mrs Patel by Friday." / "Put on everyone's list: van MOT on the 20th."
- "Tell Tom and Dan the van is in for its MOT Thursday." (each gets their own copy)
- Signed in with the owners' code, "me" and "Scott" both mean the owners' list, and the staff app's For box offers Scott (me). It answers with what it did, for example "Added to Scott's to-do list."
- "What have I asked other people to do?" / "What did we finish this week?"
- "I've rung Laura." (it ticks that one off) / "Put the Laura one back on the list."

If the assistant tries to change something and it doesn't go through (a booking, a quote line, a payment), it saves what you asked and the error to your own list under **Failed**, and tells you, so you can sort it out later.

The morning digest also says how many to-dos are open for each person and how many are overdue.

## Phone notifications

In the staff app on your phone, tap **Turn on notifications** at the top. You'll get a buzz for new enquiries, quotes accepted online and new ratings (low ones flagged), only for your own businesses. Tap it again to turn them off on that phone.

To set it up once, run `npm run vapid-keys` and paste the three lines it prints (`VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `VAPID_SUBJECT` with your own email) into Vercel's environment variables, then redeploy. Without them the button stays hidden and nothing is sent.
